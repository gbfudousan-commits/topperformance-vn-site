關於Top Performance Vietnam

傳承專業，立足越南，放眼全球

Top Performance Vietnam 成立於2024年，是台灣唐洲企業旗下的全資子公司。我們挾帶母公司超過39年的家具製造經驗，在越南太平省投資千萬美元，建立了一座70,000平方米的垂直整合製造基地。

我們不僅僅是製造商，更是客戶的策略夥伴。透過整合越南在地的供應鏈優勢與關稅利益，我們為全球客戶提供兼具成本效益與高品質的OEM/ODM服務。

鈑金加工

板材加工

全尺寸注塑

120至1,250噸伺服液壓精密注塑機。加工PP、ABS、尼龍、PC及玻纖複合材料。模具壽命超過50萬模次。

粉體塗裝

商品介紹

This height-adjustable rolling whiteboard is designed for diverse teaching and meeting needs, combining adjustable height with a sturdy structure, allowing users to flexibly adjust according to occasion and needs. The whiteboard surface features a high-quality scratch-resistant coating, smooth writing, easy erasing, ensuring it remains clear and tidy after long-term use. The sturdy metal frame with a stable base, equipped with swivel casters and brakes, facilitates movement and fixing, enhancing usability. The height-adjustable design is particularly suitable for users of different heights; whether for teacher lecturing, student presentations, or meeting presentations, it can be easily handled. Its simple and neat appearance can blend perfectly with various spaces, whether in classrooms, offices, or training centers, each complementing the other. This is not just a whiteboard, but a professional tool to enhance interaction and efficiency.
